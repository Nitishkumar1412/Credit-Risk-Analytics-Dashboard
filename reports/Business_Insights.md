# Business Insights

## Insight 1: Loan Portfolio Overview

### Observation

- Total Loan Applications: **307,511**
- Loans Successfully Repaid: **282,686 (91.93%)**
- Loan Defaults: **24,825 (8.07%)**

### Business Interpretation

The loan portfolio is generally healthy, with most customers successfully repaying their loans. However, approximately 8% of customers default, representing a significant financial risk.

### Recommendation

Future predictive models should focus on identifying this small group of high-risk borrowers before loan approval. Due to the class imbalance, evaluation metrics such as ROC-AUC, Recall, Precision, and F1-score should be prioritized over overall accuracy.
